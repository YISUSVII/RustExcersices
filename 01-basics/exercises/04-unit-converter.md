# Exercise 04 — Unit Converter

- **Title:** Unit Converter
- **Objective:** Convert kilometers to miles and back.
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Concepts practiced:** constants, floating-point operations

## Instructions

1. Define a conversion constant.
2. Implement `km_to_miles` and `miles_to_km`.

## Expected behavior

- `1.0 km` ≈ `0.621371 miles`

## Optional hints

- Use a `const` for the ratio.

## Bonus challenge

Add meters ↔ feet conversion.
