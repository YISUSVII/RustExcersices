# Exercise 01 — Temperature Converter

- **Title:** Temperature Converter
- **Objective:** Convert Celsius to Fahrenheit and Fahrenheit to Celsius.
- **Difficulty:** Beginner
- **Concepts practiced:** variables, numeric types, functions

## Instructions

1. Implement two functions:
   - `c_to_f(celsius: f64) -> f64`
   - `f_to_c(fahrenheit: f64) -> f64`
2. Print both conversions for sample values.

## Expected behavior

- `0°C` becomes `32°F`
- `212°F` becomes `100°C`

## Optional hints

- Formula: `F = C * 9.0 / 5.0 + 32.0`

## Bonus challenge

Round results to 2 decimal places.
